How Sourcework stores your data

Question this answers: when a user creates a project, imports a file, asks a question, exports fields or deletes something, which of the three stores change, and do they stay in step (also when an upload fails halfway)? The three stores are the project list (data/projects.json), the parsed text files (data/documents/<id>.json) and the search index (Pinecone, one namespace per project). Click buttons below. Boxes that just changed light up in green. Nothing here touches the real app.

1. Data model

No real foreign keys: the app's own code keeps the three stores matching. Chat history and export results are never saved.
